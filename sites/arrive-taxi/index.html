FAQ

Good to know.

How far in advance should I book?

For rides right now, just book or call — we usually arrive within 10–15 minutes. For airport runs and early mornings, booking the day before guarantees your car.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We track your flight and adjust pickup time automatically. The first 30 minutes of waiting after landing are free.

How can I pay?

Card, cash, Apple Pay and Google Pay are all accepted in every car. Business accounts are invoiced monthly.

Do you have child seats?

Yes. Mention it when you book and we’ll send a car fitted with the right seat at no extra cost.

Can I cancel?

Cancellation is free up to 1 hour before a scheduled pickup. For ride-now bookings, just call or text us before the driver arrives.
