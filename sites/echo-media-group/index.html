03 — ProcessHow we work

From whisper to echo.

Every project moves through four stages, so you always know what’s happening and what’s next.

  1. Listen

    Week 1

    We learn your business, audience and goals, then agree on what success looks like.

  2. Shape

    Weeks 2–3

    Strategy, concepts and direction. You see ideas early and steer before we build.

  3. Make

    Weeks 3–6

    Design, build, shoot and edit — with regular check-ins and no surprises.

  4. Amplify

    Launch & after

    We launch, measure what lands and keep improving so the work keeps carrying.
