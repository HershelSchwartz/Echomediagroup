We opened Ember & Salt because we wanted to cook the way you cook for friends: one menu, made that day, for a table who trust you.

There is no gas in our kitchen. Everything is cooked over oak, or in the embers it leaves behind, which means the menu follows the fire as much as the season.

We write a new menu every Tuesday, after the farm deliveries arrive, and print it that afternoon. If something runs out, we cook something else and tell you why.

Thirty-two seats is as many as one fire can feed well. Book ahead if you can. If you can’t, come and ask at the door: there is often a seat at the counter.

Sam Hollis

Before you come

AllergiesCan you cook around an allergy?

Yes, if you tell us when you book. The menu is written on Tuesday, so we can plan around most allergies with a day’s notice. Our kitchen handles nuts, gluten and dairy every day.

VegetarianIs there a menu without meat or fish?

Every night. It is six courses, cooked over the same fire, at the same price. Vegan is possible with notice.

ChildrenCan we bring children?

Children are welcome at the 6 pm sitting. We will cook a smaller plate of what they like from the week’s ingredients, at half price.

GroupsHow many can sit together?

Up to eight at one table. For more, book the whole room: all thirty-two seats, Wednesday or Thursday, by email.

Walk-insCan we come without booking?

Six counter seats are kept for walk-ins each sitting. Arrive close to 6 pm or 8.30 pm and ask.

CancellingWhat if our plans change?

Let us know 24 hours before and there is nothing to pay. We buy for the number booked, so later than that we charge £20 a head.
