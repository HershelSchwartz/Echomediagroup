How a project runs

  1. First, the idea.

    A call, a page of notes or a voice memo is enough. We listen for the one thing it has to say.

  2. Then we record it.

    Our virtual studio comes to you: remote crews, remote microphones, one producer on the line the whole time.

    Pick a room, then clap. Every space sends a sound back differently.

    A recording booth sends a sound back in 35 ms; a canyon takes nearly half a second. We choose the room before we press record.

  3. We cut and mix it.

    Picture and sound are finished side by side, so your film and your podcast sound like the same brand.

  4. It carries.

    Delivered in every size and format you need, with captions, cut-downs and a sound logo to keep.
